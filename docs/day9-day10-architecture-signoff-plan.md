# Day 9-10 Plan: Architecture Finalization and Leadership Sign-off

## Objective
Finalize architecture, environment strategy, and governance decisions so Sprint 1 can start without ambiguity.

## Outcomes by End of Day 10
- Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) approved for core platform choices
- Environment rollout and release governance approved
- Risk register and mitigation ownership ratified
- Leadership sign-off package completed

## Participants
- Engineering Manager (facilitator)
- Tech Lead / Architects
- Platform/DevOps Lead
- Security Champion
- QA Lead
- Product Owner
- Leadership stakeholders (Director/VP Engineering)

## Day 9 Agenda (Architecture Decisions)

### 09:00-09:45: Architecture baseline review
- Reconfirm system context and MVP service boundaries.
- Validate assumptions from Day 3-8.

### 10:00-11:30: ADR decisions
- Runtime/framework decisions.
- Data storage and caching decisions.
- Auth/RBAC and audit strategy decisions.
- Connector ingestion strategy decisions.

### 11:45-12:30: NFR targets
- Confirm performance, reliability, security, and observability targets.

### 13:30-15:00: Deployment model
- Confirm environment topology (dev/test/stage/prod).
- Confirm CI/CD gates and rollback process.

### 15:15-16:30: Risk and contingency review
- Validate top technical and delivery risks.
- Confirm mitigation owners and due dates.

### 16:30-17:00: Day 9 checkpoint
- ADRs ready for leadership readout.

## Day 10 Agenda (Leadership Sign-off)

### 09:00-10:30: Readout walkthrough
- Present architecture, backlog, testing approach, and rollout plan.

### 10:45-12:00: Decisions and approvals
- Resolve open tradeoffs and approve MVP execution scope.

### 13:00-14:30: Operating cadence
- Confirm weekly governance cadence and KPI review rhythm.

### 14:45-15:45: Success metrics for first release
- Set go-live success criteria and adoption metrics.

### 15:45-16:30: Formal sign-off package
- Finalize owner matrix and committed timeline.

### 16:30-17:00: Sprint 1 launch handoff
- Confirm first sprint goals, owners, and kickoff date.

## Exit Criteria
- ADRs approved by engineering leadership.
- Environment and release gates approved.
- Top risks assigned with mitigation owners.
- Sprint 1 start decision confirmed.
